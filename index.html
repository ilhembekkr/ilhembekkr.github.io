About

Engineering reliable agents.

I build production-grade agentic systems for industry. My focus is AgentOps and the engineering lifecycle of multi-agent systems — the unglamorous plumbing that keeps agents reliable past the demo.

At Omundu I work on Unity Forge, an agentic AI platform for industrial maintenance across automotive, heavy industry, and oil & gas — corpora of tangled manuals, schematics, GMAO records, and 3D plans where the answer is almost never in a single chunk. Multimodal GraphRAG — graphs over text, images, and structured data together — is what we're working on next.

If you want to talk graphs, agents, AgentOps, or anything in between over coffee, get in touch.
